Privacy Policy for Ton80

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ton80: Darts Scorer & Counter ("Ton80"), provided by Nir Barzilay, handles information when you use the Ton80 iPhone application.

Ton80 is a darts scoring app for X01 and Cricket. It keeps score, suggests checkouts, offers a computer opponent and shows your statistics and match history.

Ton80 is designed so that your data stays on your iPhone. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and we do not operate a server that receives your data. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Who We Are

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • "App" means the Ton80 iOS application.
  • "We", "us", or "our" means Nir Barzilay.
  • "You" means the person using the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

Players, Matches and Statistics

To keep score and show history and statistics, the App stores the player names you enter, the matches you play (every dart or visit total), and the statistics calculated from them. This data is stored only on your iPhone. We do not receive it and cannot access it.

App Settings

The App stores your preferences locally, such as game settings, language and the score caller.

Device Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, the App's local data may be included in that backup as part of Apple's standard device backup. The App itself does not synchronize data to any cloud service. Apple's handling of backups is governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Score Caller

The optional score caller reads scores aloud using Apple's on-device speech synthesis. No audio is recorded, and the App does not use the microphone.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Ton80 Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription through the App Store. Purchases, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information. The App only checks with Apple's StoreKit whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock its features. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Support Communications

If you contact us by email, you voluntarily provide the information in your message, including your email address and anything else you choose to include. When you use the "Contact support" option in the App, the prepared email contains the App version and iOS version to help us investigate the issue; you can review or remove this before sending. We use this information only to respond to your request and improve the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

Ton80 does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, location or payment information. We do not collect usage analytics, and we do not use advertising networks, targeted advertising, cross-app tracking or advertising identifiers. The App contains no advertisements.

Crash Reports and Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and performance data. This sharing is controlled by you and Apple, and it does not identify you personally. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Third-Party Services

Ton80 relies only on Apple's platform services, such as the App Store and StoreKit for subscriptions. Apple's processing of information is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices:

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your Choices

  • You can rename players or remove them from your player list. A removed player's name stays in the matches they played until you delete those matches.
  • You can delete individual matches from the match history.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.
  • You can delete the App to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Data Retention and Deletion

The App's data stays on your iPhone until you delete it in the App or delete the App. Because we do not keep a copy of your data, deleting the App (and any device backups that contain it) removes it permanently. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request.

Security

The App's local data is protected by the security features of iOS. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Ton80 is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. The updated policy becomes effective when it is published.
